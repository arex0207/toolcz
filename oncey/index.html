A Familiar Voice for Every Little Adventure

Oncey helps parents, grandparents, and caregivers create personal bedtime stories for a child—then bring them to life in a familiar voice, with that loved one’s clear permission. It is a comforting way to stay close at bedtime, whether you are across the room or across the country.

Make Bedtime Feel Closer

A familiar voice can make nighttime feel safer and more reassuring. Oncey supports meaningful bedtime routines when a parent is traveling, working late, deployed, living in another home, or simply wants to share a story in a more personal way.

Stories Made for Your Child

Create personal adventures

Choose a child’s age, interests, favorite animals, imaginative worlds, and the lessons you want to share. A playful interview turns those details into a story made especially for them.

Keep loved voices close

A parent, grandparent, or caregiver can record a short voice sample after agreeing to participate. Once ready, that family voice can narrate stories a child returns to again and again.

Play together as a family

Oncey also makes interactive family plays. It narrates the story, pauses for a parent’s and child’s lines, and turns screen time into shared playtime.

Revisit favorite moments

Completed stories live on the Story Shelf, where families can listen again, mark favorites, and revisit the adventures that become part of a bedtime routine.

Made for Families

  • Parents who want a calmer, more connected bedtime routine.
  • Children who have trouble settling down at night or feel separation anxiety.
  • Traveling parents who want to stay present from afar.
  • Grandparents and caregivers who want to share meaningful stories with a child.
  • Families who want to encourage imagination and a love of stories.
