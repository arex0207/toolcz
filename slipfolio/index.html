Capture Expenses Quickly

Scan and review receipts

Use your iPhone camera to scan a paper receipt, then review the recognized merchant, date, and amount before saving.

Record every expense

Add manual expenses when there is no receipt, and attach the business purpose, category, job, payment details, and any supporting image.

Find records when needed

Search by merchant, business purpose, or job. Filter expenses by job, category, or month, and review monthly totals and tax-category summaries.

Keep records local

Receipt records stay locally on your device. No account is needed to capture, organize, or preview your information.

Preview Your Tax Package for Free

Choose a tax year and preview the files Slipfolio can prepare for your accountant. Before exporting, review annual totals, category summaries, missing information, and receipt coverage so you know what needs attention.

Lifetime Export, One-Time Purchase

Recording, organizing, and previewing remain free. A single non-consumable in-app purchase permanently unlocks sharing and export for every tax year—no subscription and no annual renewal.

  • Detailed CSV expense records
  • A multi-page PDF expense report
  • An organized ZIP archive of receipt images
  • A missing-information report for records that need attention
  • A complete local backup export for moving or safeguarding records

Slipfolio is an organizational tool. It does not provide tax, legal, accounting, or financial advice, and it does not determine whether an expense is deductible. Review your records and consult a qualified professional when needed.
