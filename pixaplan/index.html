AI Extracts, You Review

PixaPlan uses AI to identify schedule details and to-dos from the text and images you share. Before anything is added, you can review the extracted items, check their details, and confirm what belongs in your plan.

  • Schedules capture a specific time and place for an event or trip detail.
  • To-dos keep actionable work separate, with an optional due date or reminder.
  • One message can produce more than one item, so a full itinerary or task list does not need to be entered line by line.

Keep the Day in View

After confirmation, schedules and to-dos are kept in one clear planning space, helping you move from a screenshot or message to a plan you can actually use.
