What It Solves

Many language tools are too broad for focused voice practice. SpeakLoop solves this by centering short-loop drills, faster repetition, and reduced friction between listening and speaking.

Who It Is For

  • English learners improving rhythm and pronunciation.
  • Users who prefer repeat-loop speaking drills on iPhone.
  • People who want practice-oriented voice workflows over media browsing.
